# Publishing checklist

Do not publish the original research PDF without reviewing personal contact information, address, student number and any signatures. A redacted public candidate exists in the prepared archive, but is intentionally not committed here until the repository owner verifies the redaction.

Do not commit: raw licensed ADE20K or Cityscapes assets, personal research data, internal documents, proprietary model weights, API keys, local environment files, or large output checkpoints.

The 4-panel street scene screenshot includes an overlay indicating another segmentation model; do not label it as a MAE-ViT output without evidence. Other illustrative input/masked/reconstruction diagrams may be included after permissions and original attribution are reviewed.

This repository currently contains no explicit software license. The owner should select one after confirming source and figure rights. No license should be presumed.
